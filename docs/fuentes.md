**If a source doesn't respond**: `ingest/http.py` retries three times. If it still can't connect to the host, it marks it as down for 30 minutes (`HOST_DOWN_PAUSE`): the other downloads from that host fail at once with "not responding" and the ingest carries on with the other sources. *Data* shows the reason and the last good data. Real case: **football-data.co.uk refused connections from 21-10-2026 at 10:47 (Lima) until 23-10-2026** (it came back that night; the data for those days was downloaded and matches FotMob), over both IPv4 and IPv6. It didn't open from outside this network either and there were no public notices, so it was the site (hosted by IONOS) and not a block on this IP. It coincides with PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, two critical PHP core flaws published on 21-10 around 14:00 UTC): the outage started at 15:47 UTC and that day IONOS announced security maintenance of its PHP services. Since the server didn't even accept connections, it was most likely switched off or isolated to patch it; that isn't confirmed. When it came back, it was worth looking at the discrepancies with FotMob in the first results downloaded (`store.py` logs them) in case the files had been tampered with. While it was down, the bet365 odds for upcoming matches (`fixtures.csv`), the closing odds of new matches (the market ratings kept the earlier ones) and the extra leagues' results were missing. The results and stats of the main leagues arrived anyway through FotMob.

**FotMob line-up types** (`content.lineup.lineupType`, checked in the cache on 22-10-2026): `standard` is the official XI (the one for played matches and the one that comes out an hour before), `predicted` the predicted one and `lastStarting11` the team's last starting XI, which FotMob shows a few days before when it doesn't have a predicted one yet (`source = lastStartingLineups`). Only `standard` is saved as confirmed (`fotmob.LINEUP_CONFIRMED`); the rest as predicted, and an unknown type leaves a warning in the log.

**Europa League and Conference on FotMob** (checked on 21-10-2026): their matches have the same Opta stats as the Champions League ones (shots, on target, corners, fouls, cards, xG) and the referee. Only played matches are downloaded (`fotmob_stats` in `core/competitions.py`), with `store.apply_fotmob_details(stats_only=True)`: result, referee and team stats, without line-ups, absences or player stats (the rotated XIs of these cups would change the usual XI of the league teams). That's about 1,020 matches since 2023-24, one request each, which stay in the cache forever; the worker downloads them little by little with each day's history. 24 of them are missing the referee.

**Serie A and Ligue 1** (full leagues since 21-10-2026). They used to be "light" leagues: from FotMob only the calendar and the result were downloaded, and from football-data the counts, the closing 1X2 and the odds (no xG until 2026-27). Now they're downloaded like the other full leagues: FotMob details (xG, stats, line-ups, absences, referee and players), Understat xG (`Serie_A`, `Ligue_1`), the odds for upcoming matches from `fixtures.csv` and Betano and Pinnacle odds through OddsPapi (tournaments `serie-a` and `ligue-1`, in the same request as the other leagues). The history is 2,191 played matches since 2023-24, one FotMob request each, which stay in the cache forever; all of them have xG, stats, referee, XI and players. Understat links all but 3 (suspended or postponed and replayed on another day), and no new duplicate teams appear across sources.

**Portugal and Belgium** (full leagues since 29-10-2026). They used to be "light" leagues from football-data only (FotMob didn't download them). Now they have the same as Serie A and Ligue 1 minus Understat, which doesn't cover them: FotMob details (leagues 61 and 40), the odds for upcoming matches from `fixtures.csv` and Betano and Pinnacle odds through OddsPapi (tournaments `liga-portugal` and `pro-league`; with eight competitions, almost every day it's 2 requests per bookmaker). The 2,014 existing matches had been created by football-data with its team names; FotMob reuses them (same pair of teams within 40 h) instead of duplicating them, and nine teams needed a variant in `names._TEAM_SEEDS` ("Standard" = "Standard Liège", "Waregem" = "Zulte Waregem", OddsPapi's "Royal Charleroi SC" = "Sporting Charleroi"…). Belgium has a regular season and playoffs: football-data and FotMob have both, without duplicates, and FotMob adds the final of the European playoff of each season. The history is 2,018 played matches since 2023-24, one FotMob request each (cached forever); almost all of them have xG, stats, XI and players, and Belgium is missing 32 referees from 2024-25 and 2025-26. In Portugal, FotMob and football-data differ in 31 % of team shots (FotMob +0.4 on average); FotMob wins, as in the other leagues.

**Turkey** (full league since 30-10-2026), the same as Portugal and Belgium: FotMob (league 71) reuses the 1,109 matches football-data had created, without duplicating teams (four new variants in `names._TEAM_SEEDS`: "Ad. Demirspor", "Amedspor", "Bodrumspor" and "Karagumruk"), and OddsPapi has it as `super-lig` (`turkiye`). Every match played since 2023-24 has xG, stats, XI and players; 26 referees from 2025-26 are missing. FotMob and football-data differ in 25 % of team shots (FotMob +0.2 on average) and in 13 % of fouls.

**Eredivisie** (full league since 21-11-2026, **on trial**: analysed but not in the bet of the day, `Competition.picks=False`, until the backtest justifies it). Like Turkey: FotMob (league 57) reuses the 1,233 matches football-data had created since 2023-24, without duplicating teams (24, the same as before; four new variants in `names._TEAM_SEEDS`: "Den Haag", "For Sittard", "Waalwijk" and "Zwolle"), and OddsPapi has it as `eredivisie` (`netherlands`): with ten competitions it still fits in 2 requests per bookmaker (0-10 more a month). All 1,035 matches played since 2023-24 have xG, stats, referee, XI and players. FotMob and football-data differ in 8 % of team shots and 16 % of fouls.

**National teams: UEFA Nations League** (since 10-11-2026, at the owner's request). Only FotMob covers them: football-data has no national teams, so there are no bet365 closing odds and no second xG. FotMob has four leagues (A 9806, B 9807, C 9808 and D 9809), which here are a single competition (`UNL`, `fotmob_more_ids`), with the same detail as a club league, also in D: referee, XI with formation and coach, absences, team stats (xG included) and player stats. There are five editions (2018-19 to 2026-27); with three years of history, 2024-25 (168 matches) and 2026-27 (104 played and 52 to play on 10-11) are included. As history for those national teams, only the played matches of the European qualifiers for the 2026 World Cup (`WCQ`, league 10195, 204 matches), the 2026 World Cup (`WC`, league 77, 104) and Euro 2024 (`EURO`, league 50, 51) are also downloaded, with line-ups and players so the usual XI is recent; World Cup and Euro matches are saved as a neutral venue (`Competition.neutral`; the hosts lose their home advantage). In total 683 matches and 86 national teams (UEFA's 54 and the World Cup opponents).

Things to know: (1) seasons aren't "YYYY/YYYY+1" every year, and **for a season that doesn't exist FotMob returns the current one**, so only the ones in `Competition.fotmob_seasons` are requested (every new edition has to be added there: the 2028-29 Nations League, Euro 2028…); (2) national teams are saved with `team.country = "Selección"`, so they're never matched by name with a club or merged with it (the club FC Andorra already existed: the national team is "Andorra (Selección)"); (3) a player who appears with their national team **doesn't change club** (`resolve_player` without a team: no transfer and no `current_team_id`); (4) the names are in English, as FotMob gives them, and `names._TEAM_SEEDS` has Betano's Spanish ones and OddsPapi's variants ("Türkiye", "Czech Republic", "Republic of Ireland"); since 15-11-2026 the dashboard in Spanish shows them in Spanish (`names.NATIONAL_ES`, which every new national team has to be added to); (5) friendlies aren't downloaded (they're played with substitutes); (6) in knockout matches the score includes extra time, as in the Champions League. OddsPapi has it as a single tournament (`uefa-nations-league`, `international`): during club breaks it's 1 request per bookmaker and day, about 14 per window. The first download (11-11-2026) matched the 26 matches of the next three days with Betano and Pinnacle, with no loose names.

**Players without a FotMob profile** (fixed on 30-10-2026): FotMob gives `id = 0` to players without a profile (youth players). They're ignored in line-ups, absences and stats. Before, they were all tied to the first player that came in with that id (Christof Katzmayr, a LASK substitute in the Champions League), and two in the same XI broke the details download.

**Team crests:** FotMob's public images (`images.fotmob.com`, by the team's FotMob id). They're downloaded with the polite client and kept in the cache for 30 days; the 08:30 preview downloads in advance those of the teams playing in the next 10 days. The API serves them at `GET /teams/{id}/logo`, without a key (they're public and an `<img>` can't send `X-API-Key`).

## Discarded

- **SofaScore**: answers 403 to automated requests. No attempt is made to get around the protection.
- **Opta directly**: it's paid (Stats Perform). Its data arrives through FotMob.
- **FBref**: since January 2026 it no longer has Opta's advanced data.
- **ClubElo**: the API didn't respond during development. The system computes its own team strength.
- **Scraping bet365/Betano**: it's against their terms and bet365 blocks aggressively. Aggregators are used instead.

## football-data extra leagues

They give the Champions League, Europa League and Conference opponents from those leagues (Bodø/Glimt, Salzburg, Copenhagen, Young Boys, Legia, Malmö, FCSB…) **market ratings** like those of the big leagues. They're registered in `core/competitions.py` with `fd_extra` (the code of the `new/XXX.csv` file) and downloaded in `pipeline.sync_football_data`, like the other football-data leagues.

- **Seasons**: Norway, Sweden, Finland and Ireland play from March to November (`calendar_year`): their season is identified by the calendar year ("2026"). The 2026-27 European season covers the years 2026 and 2027.
- **Time**: UK time, the same as in the main files.
- **Names**: football-data writes without accents ("Bodo/Glimt", "Malmo FF") and sometimes differently ("FC Copenhagen", "Legia", "SK Rapid"). The transliteration in `core/names.py` (ø, æ, ł…) and its known variants join them with the FotMob team. Some clubs change spelling within the same file depending on the season ("Ham-Kam"/"HamKam", "Din. Bucuresti"/"Dinamo Bucuresti"): `football_data.RENAMES` unifies them.
- **Not covered**: the leagues of the other European opponents (Czechia, Croatia, Serbia, Ukraine, Hungary, Cyprus, Israel…) aren't on football-data.
- **They aren't used for training** unless `APUESTAS_EXTRA_LEAGUES_IN_MODEL=true`: with the current model they don't improve the cups backtest (results in `docs/modelos.md`, "European cups").

## Cross-source validation

Every value from every source is saved in `stat_observation`. `store.reconcile()` builds `team_match_stats`:

- **Counts** (shots, corners, cards…): FotMob > football-data priority. If they differ, it's noted in `discrepancies`.
- **xG**: the average of the available providers (Opta/FotMob and, since 2026-27, football-data; Understat's stays in older matches). Each one is a different model, and averaging them usually predicts better than any single one.
- **OddsPapi odds**: OddsPapi sometimes puts into a match the odds of a bookmaker event with home and away swapped (Betano in Atlético - Deportivo, 24-10-2026: 1X2, positive handicaps and team goals reversed, negative handicaps fine). The response doesn't say which team each selection belongs to, so it can't be flipped back: all that bookmaker's odds for that match are discarded, including those saved earlier from OddsPapi, if (a) its 1X2 has the opposite favourite to Pinnacle's with 25 points or more of difference in the home probability, or (b) its alternative lines aren't monotonic (a more favourable handicap or a lower over can't pay more, with a 10 % margin). In the 19-10-2026 download only that match fell, out of 77 from Betano and 74 from Pinnacle.

## Responsible use

Requests at a moderate pace with a disk cache (finished matches aren't downloaded again). Personal use: raw data isn't redistributed. If it's ever monetised, a licensed source will be needed (API-Football, Sportmonks…).
