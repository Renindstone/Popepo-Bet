## What works today

- **Daily ingest** from football-data, FotMob and Understat for the Premier League, Bundesliga, LaLiga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Primeira Liga (Portugal), Pro League (Belgium), Süper Lig (Turkey), the Champions League and, from FotMob only, the UEFA Nations League (national teams, since 2026-11-10; see below) (Serie A and Ligue 1 full since 2026-10-21, Portugal and Belgium since 2026-10-29 and Turkey since 2026-10-30; before that they only gave results and closing odds to the strength scale; Understat doesn't cover Portugal, Belgium or Turkey), plus 9 extra football-data leagues (only for the market ratings of European opponents). From the Europa League and the Conference, FotMob also gives the referee and the team stats of played matches, without line-ups or players (since 2026-10-21). Duplicate teams across sources are merged automatically when it's safe.
- **Model** for goals with attack and defence strength, averaged xG and ratings implied by closing odds; stats (shots, corners, cards, fouls) and player projections. Adjustment for line-up and absences. The stats of teams with few matches are shrunk towards the average, and in their markets the probability is blended with the bookmaker's (since 2026-10-21: Aston Villa-Viking gave corners at +232 %, now at most +11 %). Shots and corners also follow the strength of the goals model, to compare teams from different leagues properly (since 2026-10-21: Betis-Feyenoord gave 4.3-5.6 corners against Pinnacle's 5.3-4.1; now 4.9-4.9). The Europa League and Conference stats go into those models (almost twice as many cross-league matches), but their xG doesn't go into the goals model because it made the Champions League worse. The split of shots, on target and corners between the two teams is moved towards the strength difference of the bookmaker's 1X2 (Pinnacle, bet365 or Betano), without changing the total (since 2026-10-22: Betis-Feyenoord gave 4.6-4.7 corners against Pinnacle's 5.3-4.1; now 5.0-4.3). Shots and shots on target also carry each **league's recent level** (30-day memory, without touching any team's strength; in the cups, that of both teams' leagues plus the cup's own deviation), because since July 2026 there are 10-20 % more shots in almost every league and the model took months to notice (since 2026-10-22: in October, 13.1 → 14.1 expected shots per team in the five leagues, with 14.3 real; player shots inherit it). Each stat's total has its own dispersion, because the two teams aren't independent (since 2026-10-29: in corners and shots one takes from the other and the total came out too wide). In goals, each league has its own home advantage, shrunk towards the common one (since 2026-10-29: Serie A, less; LaLiga, more). Also since 2026-10-29: stat handicaps carry the correlation between the two teams, fouls carry their league's recent level (not in the cups) and player props are conditional on the player playing (a bet on a substitute who doesn't come on is voided).
- **Bet of the day** at 09:00 (goals markets only), with a hit counter since the start on Today and in My bets (`GET /picks/record`; since 29-10-2026 it counts what was **announced**: the odds and value of the first time it cleared the threshold, also if a recalculation later withdraws it; since 31-10-2026, **one per day**: the one with the highest value × confidence among those that were the bet of the day that day, saved when chosen in `pick.pod_score`, even if it was later withdrawn; days still to come show a candidate that doesn't count, and Today warns if the one that counts isn't the one shown now). On 30-10 there were 4 because of recalculations and those computed the day before; migration `a34c33d7b99e` left one per day in the history with the announced value × the last confidence (the one at the time of choosing wasn't saved), with a `pg_dump` beforehand in `var/backups/antes-una-apuesta-del-dia-20261031.dump` (lost with the previous PC). The counter went from 9 of 14 (+3.69 u) to 7 of 10 (+4.07 u). On 30-10 the remaining one (PSG −1, won) is the one with the highest value times confidence; of the four, two won and two lost — and **automatic settlement** of picks and bets after each match (`post_match` task, at :15 and :45). (Figures from the previous database; on the new PC this counter starts from zero on 11-11-2026, see "Data from the previous PC, lost".)
- **Reviewers' verdict** (since 2026-11-07, ADR 0009): below the bet of the day on Today, "Bet", "Doubtful" or "Better not" with its reasons and the discussion folded (option A of three mock-ups). It's written by three Claude roles when the line-up is confirmed (For, Against and Judge, Sonnet with high effort), or 30 minutes before the match if it doesn't arrive. The confidence against the owner's 80 % and the streak are computed by the code. It's an opinion: it doesn't change the probability, the threshold or which bet is picked. With the AI switched off it isn't written or shown. Still without a real review: how long it takes and how much of the subscription it uses still has to be measured.
- **Bets that clear the threshold** (since 2026-10-22): every value bet that clears the bet of the day's threshold is marked when saved (`pick.qualifies`), comes first in Today's "Value" and is settled automatically. That list can also be sorted by probability, confidence or expected value (remembered in the browser) and doesn't show odds below 1.20 (`value_list_min_odds`; they're still saved, but never clear the threshold). They have their own counter on Today and in My bets, with those of the last 60 days and their result (`GET /picks/qualified`). Those saved before 22-10 were marked with the threshold in force that day: on 22-10 they stood at 6 hits out of 15 (40 %), −4.55 units. Since 2026-10-28 each one comes with its **CLV** (Today, "Value", and My bets): the odds taken against Pinnacle's fair (no-vig) odds before kick-off, or the closing odds of Pinnacle, Betfair or bet365 (`picks.closing_reference`), with where it comes from and how many minutes before the match, or the reason if it has none. About 15 min before each kick-off time with open bets that clear the threshold, the worker downloads Pinnacle through OddsPapi (at most 3 times a day). CLV was filled in for everything settled: on 28-10 all 40 that clear the threshold have it, with an **average CLV of −3.5 %** (9 of 40 positive; 20 hits out of 40, −3.85 u). (Figures from the previous database; on the new PC this counter starts from zero on 11-11-2026, see "Data from the previous PC, lost".)
- **National teams: UEFA Nations League** (since 2026-11-10, owner's decisions below): leagues A, B, C and D as one competition (`UNL`) in Matches, the match analysis (line-ups, absences, probabilities, stats and odds), Evaluate odds, the API, the MCP and the bot, with Betano and Pinnacle odds from OddsPapi. **It doesn't enter the bet of the day or clear the threshold** and **has no player markets**; its value bets are listed, with the reason written and the lowest confidence. History: Nations League 2024-25 and 2026-27, European qualifiers for the 2026 World Cup, the 2026 World Cup and Euro 2024 (683 matches, 86 national teams, all with details). Teams are stored with FotMob's English name ("Spain", "Turkiye") and can also be found in Spanish ("España vs Chequia"); since 2026-11-15 the dashboard in Spanish shows them in Spanish ("Islas Feroe vs Moldavia", "Tarjetas de Moldavia"): the API swaps the names in `names.NATIONAL_ES` in its responses to `Accept-Language: es` (`i18n.spanish`). The bot and the MCP keep the English name. Playing for the national team doesn't change the player's club or their club projections. Measured (`docs/modelos.md`, section 7): the club models don't change (1X2 0.9674 before and after) and the national teams one could only be compared with "equal teams" (0.952 against 1.099 in 2026-27), not with the bookmakers.
- **Betano and Pinnacle odds** with OddsPapi at 08:30 (one request per bookmaker and day with the competitions playing in the next 3 days; the API allows 5 per request, so with Portugal, Belgium and Turkey almost every day it's two: about 70-112 a month, and with the catalogues and CLV about 125-217 of 250), discarding the ones that come with home and away swapped.
- **Offsides** (since 2026-10-30): one more stat in the count model (how often each team is caught and how often it catches the opponent), with its split in the Stats tab, the over/under markets for the match and each team in Evaluate odds and the trap warning (the opponent is caught 1.6 times the average or more: Barcelona, Westerlo, Aston Villa…). It beats the average (+0.046 per match on the total). OddsPapi doesn't bring those Betano odds.
- **Unusual starters** (since 2026-10-30): the label "Unusual: N of 10" in the line-ups, Players and Today's Props for a starter who started 2 or fewer of their team's previous 10 matches. Measured: they shoot 14 % less than their earlier rate, not more, and the model already accounts for it (honest test without improvement); it only informs.
- **Each team's schedule** (since 2026-10-30): previous and next opponent, whether they're big teams or Champions League games, and the warning "Small opponent between big matches" on the match page and in Evaluate odds. Measured: in those matches cards (−17 %) and fouls (−5 %) go down, not shots or goals, but it wasn't confirmed with the following season, so it's only a warning.
- **Coach and formation of each match** (`match.lineup_info`, from FotMob): complete since 2023-24 in the eight leagues and the European cups. Those of the Premier League, the Bundesliga, LaLiga and the cups (5,015 matches) were filled in on 2026-10-30 from the FotMob cache, without new requests.
- **Derbies and rivalries** (since 2026-10-30): 46 rivalries in the `rivalry` table (`core/rivalries.py`), with their label on Today, Matches, the match page and Evaluate odds, the history of the last 6 head-to-heads in the Stats tab and more expected fouls (×1.08) and cards (×1.25), measured with 182 derbies and with a backtest.
- **Price comparison** (since 2026-10-29): Today's **Against Pinnacle** tab lists the Betano odds that pay at least 2 % more than Pinnacle's fair (no-vig) odds, with no model (`services/compare.py`, `GET /compare`, MCP `comparar_casas`). With what was saved since 19-10 (67 matches): in goals it almost never happens; in corners and cards, often, with a CLV of +5.2 % against Pinnacle's later price (86 % positive) and a return of +7 % ± 16 % over 194 bets. Only a few weeks so far. (Figures from the previous database; on the new PC that measurement starts from zero on 11-11-2026, see "Data from the previous PC, lost".)
- **Only current odds** (since 2026-10-29): each download notes when it saw each price again (`odds_snapshot.last_seen_at`; before, a row was only saved when the price changed), and the analysis, "Value", the bet of the day and the comparison tool leave out the lines the bookmaker no longer offers. It starts to show from the 08:30 download on 30-10: odds saved before don't have that date and count as current.

- **Player markets**: goal (also 2 or more), assist and goal or assist, with xG and xA; in Bet Builders they're computed together with the result. Corners and cards in a Bet Builder come out "approximate" (since 2026-10-20). Each player's shots, on target and cards are their share of their team's, given what's expected from the team in the match, like goals (since 2026-10-22: before, they came out 15 % short in every league; with the January model, 0.85 → 1.00 of the real figure in shots and 0.86 → 1.01 on target). When the match doesn't have any line-up yet, each player's minutes are their average over their team's last 8 matches (with 0 in those they didn't play), scaled to the 988 minutes a team plays (since 2026-10-22: before, players added up to 1,300-1,400 minutes and 20-31 % more goals and shots than their team; now 0.99, almost the same as with the confirmed XI). "Player to be booked" (since 2026-10-22): the probability is λ, not 1 − e^(−λ) (FotMob gives at most one yellow per match), a straight red counts as it's settled, the share is shrunk more towards their position and substitutes count with 20 more minutes; team cards also carry each league's recent level. Out of sample, starters 0.965 → 0.995 of the real figure (January) and 1.016 → 0.996 (July), with better log-loss in every league; team cards 1.04 → 1.01 and 1.08 → 1.00 (Serie A 1.12 → 1.07 and 1.17 → 1.05).

Only FotMob's official XI (`lineupType = standard`) counts as confirmed; the last starting XI FotMob shows when it doesn't have a predicted one yet (`lastStarting11`) and any new type are saved as predicted (since 2026-10-22: before they were saved as confirmed, the dashboard said "Confirmed" days before, the confidence rose to that of a confirmed XI and the pre-match check skipped them; 9 matches of 25-10 were fixed, including Barcelona-Real Madrid, and the labels of their saved bets).
- **Team crests** next to their names on Today, Matches, the match page and the line-ups (from FotMob, cached).
- **Editable starting bankroll** in My bets (`PUT /bankroll`), with the current bankroll next to it.
- **MCP server** for Claude, **REST API** and **Telegram bot** (the latter not configured).
- **Installation on other machines** (since 2026-10-28): `scripts/instalar.sh` does everything with one command (Python environment, own database, tables, dashboard and, the first time, history and models; it can be repeated and is used to update) and `docs/instalacion.md` goes by system: Arch, Fedora, Debian, Ubuntu, Mac and Windows (with WSL). On a Mac, without systemd, `scripts/arrancar.sh` starts everything in a terminal and Ctrl+C stops it. `scripts/db.sh` finds PostgreSQL outside the `PATH` (Debian, Ubuntu, Homebrew and the Windows installer) and works on a Mac. Since 2026-11-15 it also works on **Windows without WSL** (Git Bash): `db.sh` creates the database without a Unix socket and with the `builtin` locale, `instalar.sh` uses `py -3.14` and `.venv/Scripts`, and it's started with `scripts/arrancar.sh` as on a Mac. Tested on Linux with a clean copy, on Fedora and on Windows 11 without WSL; not yet on Debian, Ubuntu, Mac or Windows with WSL.
- systemd user services: `apuestas-db`, `apuestas-worker`, `apuestas-api` and `apuestas-web`. If the computer was off at the time of a daily task, the worker catches it up on start (since 2026-10-20). If there's no internet yet at power-on, the tasks that download wait for it (up to 10 min) and, if there's still none, the catch-up tries again after 15 min without marking them as done (since 2026-10-23). The worker only runs from 05:00 to 20:00 (systemd timers; since 2026-10-26).
